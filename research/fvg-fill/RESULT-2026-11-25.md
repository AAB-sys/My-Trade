# ULTROAN: do the FVG fill signals work? (25 Nov 2026, asked by the owner after a BUY CE at 25,897.45 on 25 Nov fell about 30 points)

Data: NIFTY 50, 5-minute candles, 481 days (11 Nov 2024 to 24 Nov 2026). The page's own pipeline: the FVG levels list's gaps (static/fvg.js, 0.5 x ATR, no seed), static/fvgsig.js scanZones, 9/21 daily EMA trend filter. Found on year 1 (to 10 Nov 2025), judged on year 2.
A plain trade for the test (mine, not the page's): from the signal candle's close, stop 1 ATR against, target 1.5 ATR for, else the day's close.

## The page's rule as it is (trend filter on)
- Year 1: 120 signals, 41% won, +217 points. Year 2: 112 signals, 32% won, -507 points.
- Average move after 1 hour: -3.5 / -5.2 points. BUY CE alone: 118 signals, 49% up after 1 hour, -7.8 points on average, worst dip -70 points on average.
- Trend filter OFF: year 1 +784, year 2 +216 (242 and 239 signals; 43% / 40% won): positive twice but small (about +0.9 point a signal in year 2): not a proof.

## Filters tried (all single features and all pairs): 301 variants
Time of day (4), age of the gap at its fill (3), gap size in ATR (3), how far the wick went through the gap (3), the fill candle's colour (2), where it closed in its range (2), where the entry stands in the day's range (3), the day's opening gap (3), side (2).
- Positive in BOTH years with at least 25 signals a year (plain trade AND move after 1 hour): **0 of 301**.
- If the signals were coin flips, about 0.4 such combinations would show by luck alone (200 shuffles of the two years).

## Verdict
No filter found. The FVG fill signal has no proven edge on NIFTY 50; keep it as a study, not an order. The 9/21 EMA trend filter did not help (year 2 worse than without it).
PR #312 (ATR seed) adds 12 BUY CE and 11 BUY PE signals over the two years, all early in the day; the early BUY CE group is the weakest (38% up after 1 hour, -16.4 points on average): not recommended.
