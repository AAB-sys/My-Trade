# What the Volume Profile page's data says so far

Updated 20 Nov 2026. N = 22 days with real futures volume (a rolling window, now 21 Oct to 20 Nov), three indices. "Early": 10 to 30 days. Full tables: `study-2026-11-20-22-days.md`. The three indices move together, so the case counts overstate the evidence.

1. STRONGER above, a coin below: above the VAH the price did not touch the VAH again in the next 30 minutes 61% of the time (919 candles; two years 49%). Below the VAL it is 49% (611; two years 51%).
2. STRONGER, but not a trade yet: a 5-minute close beyond an edge keeps going 71% of the time against 23% coming back (335 cases; two years 53% against 22%). Trading it to 15:15 showed no edge (research/V1b).
3. WATCH: a wick through an edge that closes back inside comes back 61% against 34% (297 cases; two years 51% against 22%). It reaches the POC in 60 minutes 50%. It fails on trend days.
4. NO EDGE: yesterday's VAH, POC and VAL at the first touch: went through 56%, turned 43% (95 cases; two years 47% and 43%). One row stands out and is early: yesterday's VAL touched from above went through 76% (17 cases; two years 49%).
5. NO EDGE: a close across the POC: went on 49%, came back 43% (379 cases).
6. NOT CONFIRMED: the noon rule (outside the value area at 12:00 closes outside on the same side) is 56% (32 cases) against 66% on two years.
7. FADING: when the price is outside the value area at noon, the afternoon came back 59% (32 cases), down from 69% two days ago; two years say 47%. Likely no rule.
8. NOT CONFIRMED: the busy side. Closes beyond the edge on the busy side went on 74% (82), away from it 82% (34).
9. WATCH: in a P shape, a price between the VAL and the POC goes back up to the POC 54% and down to the VAL only 19% (278 candles; two years 47% and 33%).
