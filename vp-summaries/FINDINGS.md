# What the Volume Profile page's data says so far

Updated 17 Nov 2026. N = 20 days with real futures volume (19 Oct to 17 Nov), three indices. "Early": 10 to 30 days. Full tables: `study-2026-11-17-20-days.md`. The three indices move together, so the case counts overstate the evidence.

1. STRONGER (holds on the volume days and on two years of price): once the price is above the VAH or below the VAL, it usually stays out. Above the VAH it did not even touch the VAH again in the next 30 minutes 63% of the time (805 candles; two years 50%); below the VAL 56% (630; two years 53%).
2. STRONGER, but not a trade yet: a 5-minute close beyond an edge keeps going 73% of the time against 20% coming back (274 cases; two years 53% against 22%). Caution: the close is already past the edge, so this is easier than it looks; trading it to 15:15 showed no edge (research/V1b).
3. WATCH: a wick through an edge that closes back inside comes back 59% against 35% (217 cases; two years 51% against 23%). But it reaches the POC in 30 minutes only 38% of the time (two years 55%): the POC is too far to be the target.
4. NO EDGE: yesterday's VAH, POC and VAL at the first touch: went through 54%, turned 45% (76 cases; two years 48% and 42%). A coin.
5. NO EDGE: a close across the POC: went on 49%, came back 41% (321 cases; two years 36% and 36%).
6. NOT CONFIRMED: the noon rule (outside the value area at 12:00 closes outside on the same side) is 54% on the volume days (24 cases) against 65% on two years; and the afternoon went further only 38% of the time. On 17 Nov it failed on NIFTY 50 and SENSEX.
7. NOT CONFIRMED: the busy side. Closes beyond the edge on the busy side (P up, B down) went on 78% (58), away from it 81% (32): no difference.
